# KidneyAI Brand Specification

## Identity

KidneyAI is a medical-AI research workstation for demonstrating kidney stone detection from CT scan imagery. The visual identity uses a kidney-inspired signal mark, clinical navy depth, warm bone surfaces, and signal cyan for active model states.

- Success seafoam: `#B9E6C8`
- Warning amber: `#F4B37B`

## Typography

- Space Grotesk: headings, wordmark, product labels
- DM Sans: body copy, controls, explanatory text
- IBM Plex Mono: timestamps, filenames, confidence readouts, scan metadata

## Usage Rules

Use the generated mark as the primary product identifier; do not replace it with a generic medical cross. Keep signal cyan reserved for actions, active states, and evidence overlays. Do not describe confidence as a diagnosis probability or imply clinical certification. Generated visuals are intentionally used as demo imagery and do not contain patient identifiers.
